# Ideas parking lot
Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01

## Question

Define an Idea: free text, belongs to a project (not a draft?), linkable to many issues, surfaced beside the solution editor. Does an idea ever get "used up"? Can an idea be promoted to a suggestion-type feedback item credited to the writer? Keep minimal.

## Answer

Resolved 2026-08-28 by grilling.

- **Idea** = free text, one paragraph, belongs to the **Project** (outlives drafts). Linkable to many Issues. No tags, no status beyond "used".
- Written from a project-level **Ideas** page, or inline from an Issue's right pane (**+ idea**, auto-linked to that Issue). Not from feedback items — that's what a Suggestion is.
- Linked ideas appear under *Your ideas* in the encounter's right pane.
- Adopting an idea into a Solution marks it **used in Draft N**; used ideas stay listed, dimmed.
- No promotion of ideas into suggestions.
- Exports: not in the Action Plan (the solution already carries them); the Obsidian export includes one `Ideas.md` per project, mirroring the writer's `Random Ideas.md`.
