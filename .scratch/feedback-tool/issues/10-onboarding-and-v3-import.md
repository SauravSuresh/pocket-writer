## Answer

Resolved 2026-08-28 by grilling.

- **No V3 vault importer.** The writer re-enters the Shaji project by hand as the tool's first real use — that shakes out the capture flow before the friend sees it.
- **Sample project ships with the app**, deletable: *Smoke Love Repeat* — a deliberately silly fake film. Characters **Akhil** and **Rithu**; in the end they die. Content: ~2 bosses, ~5 minions, 3 givers at different affinity ranks, fake quotes that teach reaction-vs-suggestion by example. Enough to show the whole loop on one screen.
- **First open = a tour of the game**, run over the sample project: log a reaction → extract an issue (N1/N2 fire) → plan a boss → cascade → BOSS DOWN → watch a giver's face change → End turn. Skippable; never shown again unless asked for from the header.
- **"How this works" page**, one screen, reachable from the header, shown once after the tour: the writer's philosophy compressed — every opinion is valid; a misunderstanding is the screenplay's fault; define how you *would* fix it even when you won't; symptoms vs seminal issues; record issues, not opinions; solve fundamentals first and watch surface issues fall.
